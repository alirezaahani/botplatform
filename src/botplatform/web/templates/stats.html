  • Only finished runs are counted. Queued and running runs are not counted anywhere on this page.
  • A run that someone watched in the live viewer (a viewer_connected event) may have been helped by a person. It is left out of Runs, the status columns and the rate, and is counted only in Watched.
  • Success rate is Success divided by Runs. Success means the status success, which for the ARCaptcha bots is a verified login. It shows - when Runs is 0.
  • The interval is a 95% Wilson interval, so a small count such as 80 of 80 is not shown as a certain 100%.
